Colombia Macroeconomic and Inflation Report 2025-2026:
- Banco de la República monetary policy interest rate stands at 9.75%.
- Annual CPI inflation rate in Colombia closed at 5.2% as reported by DANE.
- SIPSA agricultural price monitoring indicates price stabilization in rice, coffee, and basic foodstuffs.
- Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) inflows reached USD $13.2 billion driven by renewable energy and technology sectors.
