Colombia Foreign Trade and Export Analysis 2024-2026:
- Total Colombian exports in June 2026 registered a total value of USD $3,850 million.
- Exports to the United States accounted for 26.5% of total foreign sales, experiencing a 8.2% variation compared to previous period.
- Major non-mining export products include Coffee, Fresh Cut Flowers, Hass Avocado, and Cocoa.
- Main trading partners: United States, European Union, China, Panama, and Ecuador.
